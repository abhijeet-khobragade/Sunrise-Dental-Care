# Services and prices

All prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST. Final cost can change after the dentist's examination.

## Checkup and cleaning

A routine checkup with scaling and polishing costs ₹800 and takes about 30 minutes.
We recommend a checkup every 6 months.
Children under 12 get a free checkup when they come with a parent who has an appointment.

## Fillings

Tooth-coloured composite filling: ₹1,500 per tooth.
Silver (amalgam) fillings are not offered.
A filling usually takes 30 to 45 minutes.

## Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment costs ₹4,500 to ₹7,000 depending on the tooth (front teeth cost less than molars).
A crown after a root canal is extra: ₹6,000 for metal-ceramic, ₹12,000 for zirconia.
Treatment usually needs 2 visits.

## Teeth whitening

In-clinic whitening costs ₹6,000 per session and takes about 1 hour.
A take-home whitening kit costs ₹3,500.
Whitening is not recommended during pregnancy or for patients under 16.

## Braces and aligners

The first braces consultation costs ₹500 and includes an X-ray.
Metal braces: ₹35,000 to ₹45,000. Ceramic braces: ₹50,000 to ₹60,000. Clear aligners: from ₹90,000.
Monthly instalment plans are available for braces and aligners.

## Tooth extraction

Simple extraction: ₹1,200. Wisdom tooth (surgical) extraction: ₹3,500 to ₹6,000.

## Emergency visits

Emergency visit fee: ₹1,000, adjusted against treatment if treatment is done the same day.
Emergencies include severe tooth pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth.
For a knocked-out tooth, keep it in milk and come within 1 hour if possible.
