# Policies and FAQs

## Payments

We accept cash, UPI, debit cards and credit cards.
No-cost EMI is available on credit cards for bills above ₹10,000.

## Insurance

We do not bill insurance companies directly (no cashless facility).
We provide itemised bills and treatment reports so patients can claim reimbursement from their insurer.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before the appointment by calling the clinic.
There is no cancellation fee.
Patients who miss 2 appointments without informing us must pay the consultation fee in advance for their next booking.

## What to bring

Bring any previous dental X-rays, a list of current medicines, and a photo ID.
Arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to fill in the medical history form.

## Children

We treat children from 3 years old. A parent or guardian must stay with children under 18.

## Safety and hygiene

All instruments are sterilised in an autoclave, and disposable items are single-use.

## Reports and records

Patients can get copies of X-rays and treatment records by email within 2 working days of a request.
